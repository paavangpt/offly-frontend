# Aesthetic Playbook

Pick a direction and commit; swap fonts and colors as needed but keep the intent.

## Brutalist Raw
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque or Migra; IBM Plex Sans or Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Colors: charcoal and ivory with an acidic accent (#ff5c39 or #f2ff49); flat surfaces and thick borders.
- Layout: grid-breaking blocks, oversized headings, visible rules, obvious gutters.
- Motion: snappy entrances; hover states invert colors or reveal underlines.
- Backgrounds: checkerboard, dots, or a bold angled stripe.

## Editorial / Magazine
- Fonts: Canela, Fraunces, or Cormorant Garamond; Newsreader or Source Serif 4; DM Mono accents.
- Colors: muted deep ink, warm off-white, and burnt orange.
- Layout: tall columns, asymmetrical whitespace, generous line-height, hairline rules, small caps.
- Motion: slow fades and subtle parallax; stagger text reveals.
- Backgrounds: fine grain or low-opacity paper texture.

## Retro-Futuristic Neon
- Fonts: Space Mono or Bungee; Sora or DM Sans.
- Colors: dark base (#05060e) with neon accents (#61f4de, #ff6ad5, #8af5ff).
- Layout: angled cards, pill buttons, floating ribbons, console-style stats, thin outlines.
- Motion: glitch keyframes, sweep highlights, metadata marquees.
- Backgrounds: gradient mesh with grid lines or starfield dots.

## Soft Craft / Pastel
- Fonts: Clash Display or Epilogue; Manrope or Sen.
- Colors: warm pastel base (#f6ede3), clay/terracotta (#d96b52), and sage (#8ca28f).
- Layout: rounded blobs, gentle shadows, stacked cards, stitched borders.
- Motion: gentle float and hover lifts.
- Backgrounds: irregular gradient blobs and subtle noise.

## Industrial / Utilitarian
- Fonts: Compacta or Archivo Black; Barlow or Work Sans; JetBrains Mono numerics.
- Colors: cold neutrals (#101010, #e6e6e6) with hazard accents (#ffb400 or #2de1c2).
- Layout: rigid grids, labeled panels, coordinates, boxed callouts with corner ticks.
- Motion: linear transitions, sliding panels, striped progress bars.
- Backgrounds: diagonal hatch and soft inner glow.

Choose based on emotional goal, then choose fonts, colors, and a single signature gesture.
